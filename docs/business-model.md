# Business Model: Independent Concrete Crew Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7114`
- ISCO-08: `7114`
- Occupation: Concrete Placers, Concrete Finishers and Related Workers
- Social impact: local-jobs, worker-safety, construction-quality

## Customer

- concrete-placement and concrete-finishing crews
- general contractors coordinating crew subcontractors

## Offer

- job-site work-record logging (task/materials-usage/progress)
- crew and pour-timing scheduling coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to site safety officers
- concrete/materials supply-order coordination

## Scope boundary

This actor coordinates job-site SCHEDULING and LOGISTICS only. It does
not, and structurally cannot, place or finish concrete itself, and it
cannot finalize a concrete-pour/finishing-execution decision or
override a site safety officer's judgment — those decisions remain
exclusively with the humans on site.

## Revenue

- monthly coordination retainer
- per-site onboarding fee

## Trust Controls

- no coordination proposal may reference a site or worker that has not
  been independently verified (e.g. a site-safety officer's inspection
  sign-off) — a registered record alone is not enough
- surfaced safety concerns always require human sign-off; the governor
  never resolves a safety concern itself
- supply orders above the site's registered cost ceiling always
  require human sign-off
- any proposal to finalize a concrete-pour/finishing-execution
  decision, or to override a site safety officer's judgment, is a
  hard, permanent block — never overridable, never escalatable to
  human approval as if it were routine
- coordination and safety records are auditable, not editable
